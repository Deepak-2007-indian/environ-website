Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what ENVIRON (operated by K Deepak) collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Payment confirmation. When you pay, Razorpay processes the payment. We receive an order and payment reference so we can confirm it and issue your licence. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Device identifier. To tie your licence to one device, the app creates a scrambled (hashed) identifier from your computer. It cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Online mode messages. In Online mode, the text you type is sent to our server and then to Google’s Gemini model to produce an answer. We do not intentionally store the content of your messages on our server.

Offline mode

Offline mode runs on your own computer. Your offline conversations are not sent to our servers. The one-time setup downloads the Llama 3.2 model and the Ollama engine from their official sources.

How we use information

We use it only to confirm payments, issue and check licences, provide answers in Online mode, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information.

Third parties

Razorpay (payments), Google (Gemini model used in Online mode), and our hosting provider handle data as needed to provide the service, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can use Offline mode if you do not want your messages processed by a cloud model. To ask about your data or request deletion of payment records we hold, email environ.support@gmail.com.

Children

ENVIRON is meant for users aged 18 or older, or younger users who have a parent or guardian’s permission.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
