Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Cancellation

ENVIRON is a one-time ₹99 purchase for one year and does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends after one year unless you choose to pay again.

Refunds

Because ENVIRON is a digital product, we generally do not refund after use. However, we will refund the full ₹99 if:

  • you contact us within 7 days of payment, and
  • ENVIRON does not work on your Windows device and we are unable to fix the problem, or you were charged but did not receive access.

How to request a refund

Email kdfootballer2007@gmail.com with your payment ID, the email or phone number used for payment, and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 to 3 business days.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method. They usually reach your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money was deducted but the payment failed, or you were charged twice, the amount is normally returned automatically by the payment provider within a few days. If it is not, email us with the payment details.
