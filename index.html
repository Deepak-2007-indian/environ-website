Pricing

A one-time payment unlocks both Online and Offline mode for one year on one Windows device. It does not renew automatically. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards and netbanking).

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • At least 8 GB of RAM recommended for Offline mode
  • About 6 GB of free disk space for Offline mode
  • Internet for payment, Online mode and the one-time Offline mode download

How it works

  1. Download and install ENVIRON on your Windows computer.
  2. Open the app and pay ₹99 to unlock it for one year.
  3. Use Online mode right away. For Offline mode, click the download button once and ENVIRON installs the model for you.
